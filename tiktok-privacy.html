Privacy Policy (TikTok) – Hayretbaz Yükleyici

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Hayretbaz Yükleyici ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Hayretbaz" TikTok account to post the owner's own original short videos to that account. This policy explains how the app handles data obtained through TikTok.

1. TikTok services used

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. Use of TikTok is governed by the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

2. What data the app accesses

With the account owner's permission (TikTok OAuth), the app accesses only the authorized TikTok account in order to:

  • read basic account information (display name, avatar, open ID) and the creator posting settings, so the owner can confirm the correct account is used;
  • upload videos created by the account owner, with a caption, privacy level and interaction settings chosen by the owner.

The app does not access, collect or store data about other TikTok users or viewers, does not read messages, followers or analytics, and does not use any data for advertising.

3. How data is used, stored and shared

  • Use: data is used only to post and manage the owner's own videos.
  • Storage: the only stored data is the OAuth access/refresh token, kept in private storage on the owner's own computer.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with any third party.
  • Cookies: the app and this website do not use cookies or tracking.

4. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (or Manage app permissions). When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the app's storage within 7 days. Deletion requests can also be sent to the contact address below and are completed within 7 days.

5. Children

The app is used only by the adult account owner and collects no data from viewers, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be updated.
