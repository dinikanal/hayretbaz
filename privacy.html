Privacy Policy – Hayretbaz Yükleyici

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Hayretbaz Yükleyici ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Hayretbaz" to upload the owner's own videos to that channel. This policy explains how the app handles data.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. For information on how Google collects and processes data, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth 2.0), the app accesses only the authorized YouTube channel in order to:

  • upload videos created by the channel owner;
  • set video details: title, description, tags, thumbnail, audience setting, playlist and scheduled publish time;
  • read the basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel is used.

The app does not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users or viewers, and it does not use YouTube data for advertising.

3. How data is used, stored and shared

  • Use: the data above is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos.
  • Storage: the only stored data is the OAuth access/refresh token, kept in private storage controlled by the channel owner. No other YouTube data is stored.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with any third party.
  • Cookies: the app and this website do not use cookies or tracking.

4. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the app's storage within 7 days. You may also request deletion of any stored data by emailing the contact address below; the request will be completed within 7 days.

5. Children

The channel is a general-audience channel and its videos are not marked "Made for Kids". The app itself is used only by the channel owner and collects no data from viewers, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be updated.
